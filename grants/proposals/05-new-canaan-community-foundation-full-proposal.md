## 2. The organization

The Bridge Foundation for Student Athletes, based in Stamford, Connecticut, helps underserved student athletes get to college through baseball. Bridge pairs hands on recruiting guidance (college lists, coach outreach, player profiles, timeline planning and academic preparation) with the Access Fund, which covers the showcase fees, camp fees and equipment costs that end recruiting journeys before they start. Bridge also runs Pathways Finance, an eight week financial literacy program for teens.

Bridge was founded in 2023 by Dave Fisher, who founded the Elite Squad NY travel baseball organization in 2019 and has worked in sports facility management and baseball performance coaching. (The October 16 LOI said "more than 15 years." Dave withdrew that figure on October 17 and put his career start at 2015, so no number of years is stated here. TO CONFIRM 1.) Since 2019 the founder's work has guided more than 30 athletes to college; five committed in September 2026 alone. This year Bridge serves 35 athletes.

## 3. The need

Travel baseball costs an average of $3,700 per year nationally and up to $5,000 per year in the Northeast (Aspen Institute Project Play figures, checked October 13; exact citation TO CONFIRM 2). For low income families, that price alone decides who is seen by college coaches and who is not. Money is only half the barrier. The recruiting process is complicated and built on relationships, so families without guidance or money are shut out before it starts. As submitted in the LOI, no other Tri State nonprofit specializes in college recruiting guidance for baseball players specifically.

The second half of the need is Bridge's own: fundraising that runs on one person's network. Bridge has no development staff and is building a grants program. A grant that pays for athletes directly lets the program grow without waiting for that capacity.

## 4. The request

$10,000 in program support for the Access Fund, funding awards of up to $2,500 each for four lower Fairfield County student athletes in the 2026 to 2027 cycle, and the following year's awards within the June 2027 to May 2028 period. Each award covers what the athlete actually needs across showcases ($300 to $500 each), camps ($200 to $300 each) and equipment ($100 to $200). Athletes apply, Dave Fisher and board members review each application, and the board votes on funding. Every funded athlete also receives Bridge's full recruiting curriculum.
