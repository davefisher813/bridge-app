# Spectrum Digital Education Grants (Charter Communications): fit assessment

Status: NO PROPOSAL DRAFTED. The fit does not hold on the guidelines as published, for the reasons below. Nothing was submitted or sent.
Read: corporate.charter.com/digital-education/grants, on Nov 6 to 7, 2026.

## What the funder's page says

- Supports nonprofits that provide digital skills training, professional advancement opportunities, and the technology and resources needed for education.
- Eligible: 501(c)(3)s serving communities in a Spectrum market. The program must: report people impacted, population served and demographics; partner with low income rural and/or urban communities; be an existing program already serving the community; meet the community's unique needs; have a lasting, meaningful and tangible impact (examples given: a new digital site or app, expanded capacity of a digital learning center, a mobile computer lab).
- Selection criteria include: how the program addresses community needs with a focus on digital inclusion; whether it partners with community entities; whether it supports families or seniors in low income communities; financial feasibility within a one year period; data on impact.
- 2026: $1 million awarded; grants generally $2,500 to $50,000. Up to 10 percent may cover operating costs. Devices (laptops, Chromebooks) are allowed, but funds should be allocated in a diversified manner and not solely devices. Digital navigator programs are allowed. 12 month, non-renewable. The Spectrum bill and other internet service cannot be paid with the grant.
- 2026 window closed at 5 p.m. EST on February 27, 2026. Winners were notified in June 2026. The 2027 window is not posted. Questions go to DigitalEdGrant@spectrum.com.
- The IRS determination letter and Form 990 are required only from organizations that receive funding.

## Why the fit does not hold

1. The focus is digital skills and technology access. Bridge's programs are college recruiting guidance, the Access Fund (showcase, camp and equipment costs) and Pathways Finance (financial literacy). None is digital skills training, and nothing on record says Pathways Finance teaches digital skills.
2. The selection criteria centre on digital inclusion. A request for a financial literacy program would be weighed against that and would score weakly.
3. The Nov 6 sweep suggested "the digital/technology layer of Pathways Finance" as the fit. The record supports only that Pathways Finance meets on Wednesdays at 8 PM Eastern (online or in person is not on record). A devices line is allowed, but the page says funds should not go solely to devices.
4. Charter being headquartered in Stamford is true but is not an eligibility factor on the page.
