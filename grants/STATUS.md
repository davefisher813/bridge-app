## Corrections found while verifying (for Alfred)

1. Good Sports: the $299 is NOT a program fee Bridge pays. Goodsports.org/apply lists it as an eligibility standard: the applicant must charge youth a participation fee of $299 or below. What Bridge pays is shipping and handling at 5 percent of the value donated. The Nov 6 sweep and the Nov 6 draft both describe it as a fee Bridge would pay. Corrected in proposals/03. Also on that page: an evaluation after every donation, a release form, and an application with no save feature.
2. Impact100: the gates the Nov 6 sweep flagged (36 months, two years of CPA reviewed or audited statements, revenue of $250,000 or more) apply to the Transformational Grant only. The Impact Boost Award has its own lighter criteria, which Bridge appears to meet. The Boost page states no amount; the Grants Awarded page shows $5,000 awards. The Boost page lists no CPA statement requirement.
3. Spectrum: the sweep suggested a fit through the digital layer of Pathways Finance. The funder's own page does not support it (see proposals/06).
4. Years in the field: the NCCF fill sheet said "more than 15 years." Dave withdrew that on Oct 17. The YDF letter used "more than a decade." No proposal here states a number of years.
5. Board giving paid to date ($32,666.77): per the Oct 16 packet it includes payments by Lear Beyer, who left the board. Proposals use the approved figure and flag the wording.
6. The Access Fund per athlete figure: the fill sheet gives showcases at $300 to $500, camps at $200 to $300 and equipment at $100 to $200, with awards up to $2,500. Proposals use these ranges and do not state a single per athlete cost.
7. Not re-read at source: TD's and Citizens' pages. TD's Regional Grants pages and Citizens' site could not be loaded here, so those two proposals rely on Alfred's Nov 6 reading. Goodsports.org, Impact100 and Charter were read directly.
